    -> IT Asset Management is the process of Tracking and Managing an Organizations Technology Assets
    throughout their Life cycle

        -> Assets Can Include Computers, Laptops, Servers, Routers, Softwares like Photoshop etc 

        -> If Implemented Properly ITAM helps you control costs, avoid unnecessary licenses, security(audit) 
        and ensure people have the equipment they need.


    -> As an IT Asset Manager, you need to take care of 

        -> Reviewing Asset Requests and Checking Approvals 

        -> Recording Purchases and Receiving New Equipment

        -> Assigning Assets To Employees, Locations, Departments 

        -> Updating Assets Details, Asset Status 

        -> Tracking Transfer, Repairs, Returns and Retirements 

        -> Comparing Assets Records With Configuration Data 

        -> Check Software Installs and Licenses Usage 

        -> Following Up On Missing, Overdue and Unreturned Equipment 

        -> Creating Reports On Asset Counts, Cost etc
